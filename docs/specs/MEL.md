# Mandala Energy Law (MEL)

**Status:** declared  
**Runtime:** CIS `ENRG` mnemonic records a token only (**skeleton**)

## Purpose

Ensure energy consumed in the fabric is lawful, traceable, and justified.

## Foundational equation (declared math)

\[
E_L = \sum_i \frac{P_i \cdot C_i}{R_i + \Omega_i}
\]

Helpers in `src/performance.js` compute related terms; they are **not** watt-meters.

## Clauses

All MEL clauses remain **declared** until a real energy telemetry + EGC gate exists.
